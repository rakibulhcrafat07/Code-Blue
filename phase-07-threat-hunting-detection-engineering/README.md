# Phase 7 — Threat Hunting & Detection Engineering

Stop waiting for alerts and start actively looking for what your detections might be missing. This is the step from SOC analyst toward threat hunter.

**Suggested pace:** Month 4
**Difficulty:** Advanced

## Topics

- [ ] Hypothesis-driven hunting process
- [ ] Threat intel basics: OSINT, MISP, IOC feeds
- [ ] Adversary emulation with CALDERA or Atomic Red Team across a full attack chain
- [ ] Detection-as-code: keeping rules in version control (Git)
- [ ] Purple teaming — why red and blue working together beats either working alone
- [ ] Tuning detections down to fewer false positives; measuring coverage with ATT&CK Navigator heatmaps
